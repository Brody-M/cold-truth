## Material required for standard long-form viability

1. **Verified personal and professional context:** reliable first-hand, archival, or institutional material establishing Jodi's background, KIMT role, and relevant routine without relying on creator summaries.

4. **Initial search and investigation chronology:** official records or contemporaneous established reporting describing confirmed searches, agency actions, and public investigative milestones after June 27, 1995.
5. **Primary court material:** the actually unsealed portion of the 2017 warrant or docket material, used only within its public legal limits.
6. **Verified current status:** a publication-date official law-enforcement recheck of the case status and contact information.

These categories identify what the ledger lacks; this assessment does not research, add, or infer any new fact.
