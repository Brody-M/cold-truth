## Description

At roughly 4:10 a.m., Jodi Huisentruit's colleague called after she did not arrive for her KIMT morning shift. By 7:13 a.m., a welfare check had been dispatched to her apartment complex in Mason City, Iowa.

In this Cold Truth episode, we follow the documented timeline of Jodi Huisentruit's 1995 disappearance: the missed shift, the public reporting about the parking-lot evidence, and the official updates that show the case remains active. We also explain the limits of the public record, including a search-warrant affidavit that remains sealed. The story is told carefully and chronologically, with attention to what public records establish, what witnesses reported at the time, and what investigators have not made public. This episode separates verified facts from unanswered questions and does not speculate about a suspect.
