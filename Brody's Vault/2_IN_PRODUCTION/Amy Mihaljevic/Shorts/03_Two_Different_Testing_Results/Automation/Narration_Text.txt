Two testing reports in Amy Mihaljevic’s case concerned different evidence. Amy was ten when she was abducted in Ohio in 1989.

In 2024, police said retesting her clothing detected male DNA, but too little to create a profile. That did not identify a person.

Separate hairs were sent for testing in California. In January 2026, Cleveland 19 reported that Astrea Forensics could not obtain a profile from those hairs, according to Detective Sergeant Jay Elish.

That outcome concerned the hairs. It did not erase the separate clothing-DNA finding. Investigators were continuing DNA work; neither report announced an identification.
