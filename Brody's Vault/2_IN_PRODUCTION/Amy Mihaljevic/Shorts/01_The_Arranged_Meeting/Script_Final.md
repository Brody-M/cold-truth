<!-- P01 -->
A gift for her mother was the explanation Amy Mihaljevic was given for an after-school meeting.

<!-- P02 -->
Amy was ten. Before she disappeared on October 27, 1989, a caller had contacted her about celebrating her mother’s promotion. Bay Village Police say that contact happened weeks earlier.

<!-- P03 -->
The FBI says Amy walked from school to Bay Village Square in Ohio, where she was seen apparently waiting outside a business. Police describe witnesses seeing her speak with an unidentified man and walk through the parking lot with him.

<!-- P04 -->
No vehicle was seen, according to police. That sighting does not establish where Amy went next.
