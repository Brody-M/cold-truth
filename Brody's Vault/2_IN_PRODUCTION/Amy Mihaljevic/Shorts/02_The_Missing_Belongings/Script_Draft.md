<!-- P01 -->
A turquoise horse earring is one of the unrecovered belongings listed by the FBI in Amy Mihaljevic’s case.

<!-- P02 -->
Amy was ten when she was abducted from a shopping center in Bay Village, Ohio, on October 27, 1989. A jogger found her body in rural Ashland County on February 8, 1990.

<!-- P03 -->
The FBI says three items believed to have been with Amy were never recovered: that earring, black ankle boots and a black leather binder.

<!-- P04 -->
Those details are part of a public request for information. They do not establish what happened to the objects or identify the person responsible.
