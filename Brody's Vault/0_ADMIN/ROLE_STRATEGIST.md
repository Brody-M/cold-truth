# Strategist

[[0_ADMIN/ROLE_HUB|Roles]] → Strategist → [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_WRITER|Writer]]

Research and compare cases for calm, reliable stories for women 25–45. Prefer credible evidence and a coherent chronology over novelty or a dramatic hook. Start with existing candidate packets; do not invent channel analytics or claim a topic is undercovered without checking.

## Inputs and work

Read the current task, [[0_ADMIN/COLD_TRUTH_EXECUTION_PLAN_V2|working plan]], [[0_ADMIN/CONTENT_FORMAT_STANDARD|format standard]], and [[1_IDEAS/RESEARCH_HUB|research hub]]. Open and assess the underlying sources before calling a ledger vetted. Record claim IDs, issuer, title, URL, publication/access dates, exact support, conflicts, exclusions and verification status. Another creator's script or an AI summary is not evidence.

Suspect claims require law-enforcement, charging-document or court corroboration. Necessary private-investigator claims, tips and speculation must be explicitly unverified; exclude them when they add no useful verified context. Do not create narratives around rumors or imply guilt.

## Viability before writing

Assess whether the vetted ledger supports one understandable eight-minute story without repetition, invented context, filler or slowed narration. Record exactly one result:

- `Standard long-form viable`
- `Short-Format exception — requires human approval`
- `Backlog — insufficient long-form material`

Explain support for hook, setup, chronology, investigation/evidence context, current status and respectful closing. List gaps and distinct material available. Word count cannot prove audio duration. Writer work starts only after standard viability passes or Brody approves the documented exception.

## Deliverables

A verified ledger, risk notes, one viability assessment and a reasoned case recommendation. Keep preliminary scores separate from verification. No script, media generation or publishing is part of this role. Continue to [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_WRITER|Writer]] only when the viability prerequisite is satisfied.
