Prepare accurate, respectful packaging from the approved script and vetted ledger. Use the current release-master context for final timestamps and delivery settings. Keep long-form and Shorts metadata separate; a draft may be prepared locally, but cannot claim a nonexistent master is release-ready.

Return three honest title options, a clear description, source links, required asset/gameplay credits, relevant tags, two concise thumbnail text ideas and a release checklist. Avoid sensationalism, guilt implication, unsupported claims, fabricated quotes or a promise the story cannot fulfill. Thumbnail ideas are text briefs; generating final imagery requires Phase 2 authority.

Check titles and overlays against sources, and timestamps against the exact approved master. Do not make unsourced claims about search ranking, algorithm suppression, subscriber counts or monetization eligibility. Check current official platform requirements at the time of publishing preparation; old numeric thresholds in notes are not authority.

At M7, audit each title/thumbnail promise against what the approved opening and story actually deliver, and check thumbnail legibility at phone size. Derive chapters from the reviewed master rather than a generic template. After release, record the actual channel baseline for click-through rate, retention and average view duration before proposing packaging changes; no fixed percentage guarantees an algorithmic outcome. These selected checks adapt [Agency Agents' video-optimization reference](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents/blob/main/marketing/marketing-video-optimization-specialist.md) to Cold Truth's factual, calm style without importing its numerical targets or promotional persona.

Before release, verify paired script approval, narration runtime, asset rights, separate visual tracks, master QA, captions/credits, metadata and thumbnail review. Present the exact account, files, visibility and schedule for publishing authorization. Do not upload, schedule, publish, change account settings or post messages without explicit authorization. Record the verified platform result only after the authorized action succeeds.
