# Writer

[[0_ADMIN/ROLE_STRATEGIST|Verified research and viability]] → Writer → [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_EDITOR|Editor]] → [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_HUB|All roles]]

Write a calm, coherent spoken story for first-time background listeners. Require a vetted ledger and either `Standard long-form viable` or a documented human-approved Short-Format exception. Follow [[0_ADMIN/CONTENT_FORMAT_STANDARD|the format standard]] and [[0_ADMIN/COLD_TRUTH_EXECUTION_PLAN_V2|the working plan]].

## Narrative requirements

Use a purposeful hook, essential setup, chronological events, investigation/evidence context, verified current status and respectful closing. Explain why evidence matters. Restructure as needed for comprehension; a forced twist or rigid section word quota is not required. Keep the hook concise and source-bound. Do not invent scenes, dialogue, motives or personal details.

Normally draft 1,150–1,450 spoken words for the 8–10-minute target, with 8:15–10:30 preferred. At least 480.000 seconds of actual approved narration is required unless the human approves a machine-documented `short-format` exception. Neither a word count nor reading-time estimate verifies duration. Never label a script Short-Format solely because it is below a word threshold.

Every factual narration claim maps to a ledger claim ID. Suspect claims require corroboration from law enforcement, a charging document or a court finding. Label permitted uncertainty explicitly; omit unsupported or unnecessary claims. Do not borrow another creator's wording, structure or narration.

## Output

Return `Script_Draft.md`, a section/paragraph-to-claim map, spoken word count, clearly labeled runtime estimate, and exclusions. Optional `[B-ROLL: ...]` markers are long-form editorial suggestions only; they are not sourced or generated visuals and are excluded from spoken word count. Never suggest Orbital gameplay in this track.

End respectfully, without inviting theories, promising unverified revelations or claiming a release cadence that has not been established. If more length would require padding, return the gap to the Strategist.

The Editor produces the reverse outline. Brody must approve that exact outline and the full draft together before `Script_Final.md` exists. The Writer does not approve its own work or begin narration, Shorts derivation or production.
