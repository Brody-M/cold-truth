# Shorts editor

[[0_ADMIN/ROLE_HUB|Roles]] → [[0_ADMIN/CONTENT_FORMAT_STANDARD|Format standard]] → [[2_CHANNEL_SYSTEM/WORKFLOW_PER_VIDEO|Workflow]]

Begin derivation only after paired human approval of the long-form draft and reverse outline and creation of the approved final script. Use that script and the vetted ledger together; the script is not a replacement for source evidence. Follow [[0_ADMIN/COLD_TRUTH_EXECUTION_PLAN_V2|the working plan]].

Create 3–5 strong Shorts for a standard episode, or 1–2 for a human-approved Short-Format episode. Each covers one understandable, source-backed idea in a standalone script. Target 30–60 seconds; actual narration must not exceed 60.000 seconds without explicit machine-documented human approval. Word count is only an estimate.

Give each Short a factual hook, enough setup to understand it, a clear account and a respectful ending. Do not withhold essential context to manufacture mystery, invite theories, imply suspects or add unsupported claims. Do not assert algorithm rules without current evidence.

Provide a Short ID, claim IDs, complete separate narration script, `[OVERLAY: ...]` cues, estimated duration and an Orbital-only visual plan. Overlays must match the spoken facts and be readable. Measure the resulting separate narration after authorized generation; never assume the long-form audio can be reused.

Use a separately licensed Orbital gameplay background. Long-form B-roll, case graphics, visual files, source identities and derivatives cannot enter the Shorts track. A gameplay link alone does not make the timeline ready: the licensed or recorded source must exist locally with rights evidence.

This role prepares scripts and plans. Narration, media generation, assembly and render require their production prerequisites and explicit scope. [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_UPLOAD_MANAGER|Upload Manager]] prepares factual metadata after review; publishing remains a separate decision.
