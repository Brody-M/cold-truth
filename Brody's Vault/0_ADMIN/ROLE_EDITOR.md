# Editor

[[0_ADMIN/ROLE_WRITER|Draft]] → Editor → paired human approval → [[2_CHANNEL_SYSTEM/WORKFLOW_PER_VIDEO|Production prerequisites]] → [[0_ADMIN/ROLE_HUB|All roles]]

Require the full draft, vetted ledger, viability record and [[0_ADMIN/CONTENT_FORMAT_STANDARD|format standard]]. A script pasted alone is insufficient for factual review. Follow [[0_ADMIN/COLD_TRUTH_EXECUTION_PLAN_V2|the working plan]].

## Review

Check every narration claim against evidence, preserve attribution and uncertainty, and remove unsupported suspect implication. Assess dignity, privacy, calm tone, chronology, clear pronouns, useful evidence explanations, current status and a respectful ending.

Read the story as a first-time background listener. Each section must have a distinct purpose and an understandable transition. A sequence of fact cards fails even if each claim is individually supported. Repetition scanning is useful but cannot certify coherence. Request or perform a source-bound structural rewrite when line edits cannot fix the story.

Long-form targets 8–10 minutes, normally 1,150–1,450 spoken words. Projected narration below eight minutes requires a documented human-approved Short-Format exception before approval; actual approved audio must later pass the 480.000-second preflight. No padding, slowed narration, invented context or repetitive transitions.

## Paired review packet

Produce the complete revised draft, editorial notes, narrative QA, exclusions and a source-bound reverse outline. For every section the outline identifies purpose, ledger claim IDs, transition and excluded material. Present the exact draft and outline together for Brody's review.

Use `Script_Draft.md` or an explicitly versioned draft until paired human approval exists. Only then may the approved draft become `Script_Final.md` with exact input hashes and the real approval reference. A prior draft approval, filenames or editorial self-assessment do not satisfy this gate. No narration, alignment, visual sourcing, Shorts derivation, assembly or rendering begins before the approved final exists.

## Authorized audio review and handoff

When Brody requests audio editing, take the exact existing narration file as input and retain its original bytes. Measure peak level, true peak, loudness and duration before applying gentle plosive/harshness control and dynamics appropriate to that file. Do not treat loud P sounds as proof of a high-frequency fault, or remove clarity with an indiscriminate cutoff. Record the exact filter chain, tool/version, input/output hashes and all output paths in the Automation manifest; return a lossless edited copy and a listening preview.

Preserve words, pacing and pauses. Technical measurements do not approve listening quality. Brody reviews the processed audio before it becomes a video soundtrack; actual episode preflight and timing must bind that exact approved file. A neutral voice test cannot pass episode runtime or authorize assembly. Follow [[0_ADMIN/MIA_AUDIO_EDIT_2026_10_20|the current Mia sample edit]] for this bounded example.
