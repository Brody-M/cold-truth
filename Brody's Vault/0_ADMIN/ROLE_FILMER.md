# Visual producer → long-form

[[0_ADMIN/ROLE_HUB|Roles]] → [[2_CHANNEL_SYSTEM/WORKFLOW_PER_VIDEO|Workflow]] → [[0_ADMIN/CONTENT_FORMAT_STANDARD|Format standard]]

Create a long-form shot list from the human-approved final script, vetted ledger, current narration, passing duration preflight and alignment. An editor's draft is not final-script approval. Follow [[0_ADMIN/COLD_TRUTH_EXECUTION_PLAN_V2|the working plan]] and the explicit production scope.

Use only case-appropriate B-roll and case graphics. Avoid misleading depictions of identifiable people, locations or evidence. Clearly distinguish illustrative footage from genuine case material. Orbital gameplay and all Shorts visual assets are prohibited in this track.

For each shot record narration cue, aligned start/end, narrative purpose, visual description, source/asset identity, local path, license evidence and QA state. Before audio alignment, any allowed editorial cue is only an estimate and is not an assembly-ready timeline.

Prefer relevant, restrained imagery. A search result or URL is not a downloaded asset or proof of rights. Pexels clips must exist locally and be verified before the timeline is ready. Record provenance, license terms and required credit; do not add paid assets outside the agreed scope.

Planning does not authorize downloading or final visual generation. Phase 2 scope controls execution. Check cross-track IDs, source identities, content hashes and canonical paths; no shared visual files, derivatives or timelines. Hand off an accurate shot list and rights ledger, then stop at the applicable production or review gate.
