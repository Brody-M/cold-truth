## Allegations and suspect material excluded from the vetted narration pool

- Every named suspect, convicted offender comparison, jailhouse statement, alleged confession, psychic claim, internet theory, and “someone knows” accusation is excluded.
- Claims about a specific van, grave location, parking structure, anonymous caller, or alleged map are excluded as explanations.
- Criticism that police incompetence, scene contamination, or misconduct caused the case to remain unsolved requires primary records and balanced official response; it is not approved here.
- The fiction book, podcasts, YouTube videos, Reddit timelines, and creator scripts found during search were not used as sources.
- Legal-death claims are excluded until the actual court orders are obtained.

## Unknowns that must remain unknown

- What occurred inside or outside the home during the FBI’s disappearance window.
- Whether the women left voluntarily, were coerced, or were abducted; official public records do not establish the mechanism.
- Whether the three left together or separately.
- Whether any reported vehicle, call, message, letter, or person was connected.
- Where the women are and whether they are living or deceased.
- Who, if anyone, is criminally responsible.

## Draft boundary decision

This ledger supports a detailed chronology, house/absence context, initial search scale, investigative burden, family impact, and current status. It does not authorize a suspect/theory chapter or treating widely repeated scene lore as confirmed evidence.
