## Viability result

**Short-Format exception — requires human approval**

This assessment applies to the currently reverified, theory-excluding packet, not every possible future source. It supersedes the prior standard-viability conclusion for current work. The original structure allocates substantial time to overlapping absence, search-pressure and remembrance material. These do not yet justify a cohesive eight-minute treatment without repeated uncertainty or a theory chapter.

Supported sections: a concise hook; relationships/graduation setup; short chronology; attributed scene observations; search/investigation context; dated current status and respectful close. Missing depth: primary initial investigative chronology, distinctly documented follow-up actions/outcomes and more verified personal context. The old seven-chapter word ranges also total **1,210–1,410**, but assigning words does not establish distinct narrative substance or audio duration.

Recommendation: defer this candidate while the stronger Amy packet proceeds. No Short-Format exception is approved, no script is commissioned here, and no episode is created. Additional verified material could support a later reassessment.
