## Allegations and sensitive claims excluded from the vetted narration pool

- The Ohio Attorney General page says Amy was “most likely sexually assaulted.” This is probabilistic and highly sensitive. Exclude unless underlying medical examiner or case documentation is obtained and the detail is editorially necessary.
- Any named or unnamed “person of interest,” polygraph claim, lineup claim, former-partner allegation, or internet-identified suspect is excluded without an official charging document, law-enforcement statement, or court finding.
- Claims that other children received identical calls are excluded until supported by an official case record or sufficiently corroborated contemporaneous reporting.
- Claims about a specific vehicle, caller name, exact dialogue, crime location, or motive are excluded.
- Reward figures other than the current FBI’s up-to-$25,000 offer are excluded unless the responsible agency confirms the current combined amount.

## Unknowns that must remain unknown

- The caller’s identity and precise words.

- Where Amy was held or killed.

- Whether trace DNA or hair belongs to the offender.
- The identity of the person responsible.
