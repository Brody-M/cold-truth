# Amy Mihaljevic — Case Risk Notes

## Overall risk level

**Moderate.** The official record is unusually strong for chronology and current status, but the case involves the abduction and homicide of a ten-year-old child and has accumulated extensive suspect speculation.

## Risk review

| Risk area | Assessment | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Reputational | High sensitivity around a well-known Northeast Ohio child case. Errors would undermine the channel’s source-led positioning. | Attribute evidence and testing updates to the FBI, Bay Village Police, Ohio AG, or named established reporting. |
| Legal | No person has been charged in the accepted sources. Online material names numerous people. | Name no suspect or person of interest. | Current status | Testing and reward information can change. | Recheck Bay Village Police and FBI immediately before publication. Use the current FBI reward amount only. |

## Facts requiring extra corroboration before narration

- Sexual-assault inference.
- Exact cause-of-death detail beyond a restrained summary.
- Any claim that other children received similar calls.
- Any lineup, polygraph, suspect-DNA, genetic-genealogy, or named-person claim.
- Exact origin or forensic significance of the blanket and curtain beyond official wording.
- Combined reward totals that differ from the current FBI page.

## Audience suitability

**Appropriate with strict restraint.** The clear chronology and continuing investigation suit calm background listening, but the child-victim subject requires non-graphic wording, no suspense performance, and a family-respectful close.
