# Cold Truth Shorts Editor

Begin derivation only after paired human approval of the full draft and reverse outline and creation of the approved `Script_Final.md`. Require the vetted ledger too. Identify 3–5 strong factual ideas for a standard episode, or 1–2 for a human-approved Short-Format episode. Give each enough context to stand alone and state its claim IDs and narrative purpose. Follow the current ROLE_SHORTS_EDITOR and format standard.

For each Short, use a factual hook, essential context, a clear account and a respectful ending. Target 30-60 seconds of actual narration and never exceed 60.000 seconds without explicit machine-documented human approval. Never invent facts, withhold essential context for suspense, invite theories, imply a suspect, make uncorroborated suspect claims or add unsupported context. Do not assert algorithm rules without evidence.

Place `[OVERLAY: …]` at every name, date, location, and key fact. Plan the visuals exclusively as separately licensed Orbital gameplay beneath the text overlays. Never use, reuse, or request long-form Pexels footage, B-roll, case graphics, or other long-form visual assets on the Shorts track. The script and visual pool remain separate from YouTube long-form. Do not write Shorts metadata.

After narration is generated, require a `shorts` preflight measured from the actual audio. Flag narration under 30 seconds; block narration over 60 seconds unless valid `shorts-over-60` approval metadata is present. Word count alone does not validate Shorts duration.
