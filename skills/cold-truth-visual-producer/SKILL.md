# Cold Truth Visual Producer

Require paired human approval of the full draft and reverse outline, the approved `Script_Final.md`, exact current narration, passing measured-audio preflight and alignment. Follow the repository's current ROLE_FILMER and format standard. Use aligned timings; label unresolved timings provisional. Translate the approved narration without changing it. Each shot records its narration cue, purpose, timing, description, source/asset identity, path, rights evidence and QA state. Sourcing/downloads require authorized production scope.

Keep long-form visuals clean, quiet, documentary-like, and slightly dark. Use generic but relevant stock footage, text overlays for an important name/date/location only when helpful, and cuts at breath or sentence breaks. No shot should remain uncut beyond 20–25 seconds. Give the hook the strongest footage.

Never suggest graphic media, misleading depictions of real victims or crime scenes, potentially harmful identified locations, or footage that could misidentify a person. Use only case-appropriate long-form B-roll and case graphics. Shorts use a separately licensed Orbital gameplay track; no visual files, derivatives or source identities are shared. Overall visual readiness requires locally verified Pexels clips and the separately licensed or recorded Shorts gameplay under AGENTS.md.
